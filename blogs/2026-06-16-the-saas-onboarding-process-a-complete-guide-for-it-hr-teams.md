A comprehensive guide on building scalable SaaS onboarding, streamlining workflows, and automating user provisioning. Key steps include defining role-based access, provisioning accounts, assigning licenses, configuring permissions, and validating access before day one, while addressing challenges like manual provisioning bottlenecks, over-provisioning, HR-IT misalignment, and incomplete audit trails. It recommends connecting HRIS systems to identity providers, using workflow engines for automation, and zero-touch onboarding, with a checklist covering pre-day-one, day-one, and first-week tasks.
